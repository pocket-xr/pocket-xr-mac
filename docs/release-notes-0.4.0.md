# Pocket XR for Mac 0.4.0

**Steady mode, your way.** Two things a tester asked for (on Windows, with the XREAL 1S), now on the Mac too.

- **Screen gap**: choose the space between your screens in Steady mode, from touching (0°) to wide (3°). It is in the
  control panel under Size while Steady mode is on. It starts where it was, at 0.6°.
- **Steady mode is remembered**: if it was on, it comes back on by itself the next time your glasses connect or
  Pocket XR starts. Turning it off is remembered too.

Not yet tried with glasses: bringing Steady mode back on by itself, and the screen gap in the glasses. Both were
checked with the app's own checks (975 pass) on an Intel Mac. **Send logs to Mike** in the menu helps, whether it works
or not.

## Install

Download `PocketXR-Mac-0.4.0.zip`, unzip it and move **Pocket XR** to Applications. One app for Intel and Apple
Silicon, signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple. Apple Silicon has not been tried on a real M-series Mac
yet.

Pocket XR is a community project, not made by or affiliated with XREAL or VITURE.
