# Pocket XR for Mac 0.5.5

- **Smooth Follow shows a whole screen.** With two screens side by side, Smooth Follow kept the middle of the row in
  front, which is the gap between them. So only half of each screen could be seen, and because the screens follow
  your head, turning didn't help. Now, with Head scroll off:
  - Smooth Follow settles on the middle of the screen nearest where you look;
  - moving the pointer onto the other screen brings that one in front;
  - a quick turn of the head past halfway to it does too.

  With one or three screens the middle screen is in front, as before. Body Anchor and Head scroll are unchanged.
  Screens stacked one above the other are not changed. **Not yet tried with glasses**: if it looks wrong, use
  **Send logs to Mike** in the menu.

Everything else is as in 0.5.4.

## Thanks

## Install

Download `PocketXR-Mac-0.5.5.zip`, unzip it and move **Pocket XR** to Applications. One app for Intel and Apple
Silicon, signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple.
